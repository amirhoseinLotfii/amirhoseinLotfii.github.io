Kinetic Pebble Uploader — Privacy Policy

Kinetic Pebble Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Kinetic Pebble. It is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own original videos to that channel and to read that channel's own YouTube Analytics.

Data accessed: the owner's YouTube channel information, the owner's uploaded videos, and the channel's analytics, via the YouTube API Services (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).

Data use: only to upload/schedule the owner's videos and to produce private performance reports for the owner.

Data storage: OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's private computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third parties: this tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
